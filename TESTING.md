# ScentWatch 1.0 Beta Testing

Thank you for testing ScentWatch.

ScentWatch 1.0 Beta is the first external test release of ScentWatch, a fragrance price-intelligence application designed to help users understand current and historical fragrance pricing across supported UK retailers.

This beta is intended to help identify bugs, confusing behaviour, usability problems and areas where ScentWatch could be improved before future releases.

You do not need to answer every question in this document. Natural feedback is just as useful. The questions are here to highlight areas we are particularly interested in.

## Before You Start

Please pay particular attention to the introductory onboarding screens when you first open ScentWatch.

We are especially interested in whether these are genuinely useful to somebody seeing ScentWatch for the first time. Once onboarding has been completed, it will not normally appear again.

Please feel free to be critical. If something is confusing, unnecessary or simply doesn't work for you, we'd like to know.

## Suggested Test Journey

There is no required way to test ScentWatch, but the following will exercise most of the main features:

1. Complete the first-launch onboarding.
2. Browse the fragrance catalogue.
3. Add several fragrances to your Watchlist.
4. Set target prices for some of them.
5. Leave at least one fragrance without a target price.
6. Perform a manual price scan.
7. Explore the Dashboard after the scan.
8. Open Product Details for several fragrances.
9. Explore their historical pricing.
10. Change one of your target prices.
11. Remove and re-add a fragrance.
12. Explore the Notifications page.
13. Enable automatic scanning and configure the scan times if you wish.
14. Try both Light and Dark Mode.
15. Close ScentWatch completely and reopen it.
16. Check that your Watchlist, targets, settings and collected information have persisted.

You are also very welcome to use ScentWatch naturally rather than following this sequence.

If you find a way to confuse or break it, that's useful too.

# Reporting a Bug

If you encounter a bug, please provide as much of the following information as possible:

- ScentWatch version
- Windows version
- What you were trying to do
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Whether the problem happens every time
- The fragrance or retailer involved, if relevant
- Any error message shown
- A screenshot, if possible

Please do not include passwords, account information or other sensitive personal information in a bug report.

# About Beta Software

ScentWatch 1.0 Beta is a test release.

Although it has undergone internal testing, unexpected behaviour and bugs are still possible. Retailer websites and product listings can also change independently of ScentWatch, which may temporarily affect individual price sources.

Your feedback during this beta will help determine what should be fixed, changed, removed or added in future versions.
